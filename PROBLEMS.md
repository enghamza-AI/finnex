Finnex — Problem Definition
1. Target User (V1)

The target user for Version 1 is me — a university student with irregular awareness of spending and a lump-sum monthly income (allowance, stipend, or similar). I am comfortable using a command-line interface.

Future (explicitly NOT V1): other students, shopkeepers, and general middle-class users. These groups have different needs (e.g. tax/vendor tracking for shopkeepers) and are out of scope until V1 is validated on a single real user — me.
